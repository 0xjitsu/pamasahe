# Pamasahe

A free **fare calculator for Philippine commuters**: enter a distance or pick two points to see the jeepney, modern jeepney, city bus and UV Express fare, including the student, senior citizen and PWD discount. Fares come from the official LTFRB fare matrix, with its memorandum number and effective date shown on the page.

Status: being built. See the build brief in the issues.
